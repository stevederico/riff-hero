    How To Play

    1. Notes roll down five lanes. Hit each one as it crosses the strike line.
    2. Keyboard: A S D F G or 1 to 5. Phone: tap the lanes.
    3. Notes with a tail are sustains. Hold until the tail ends for bonus points.
    4. Every 10 notes in a row raises your multiplier, up to x4. A miss resets it.
    5. Hit every starred note in a phrase to charge Surge. Fill it halfway, then press Space or tap the Surge meter to double your multiplier.
    6. Misses drain the crowd meter. If it empties, the show is over.

    Settings
